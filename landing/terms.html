4. How the daily call works

  • Calls are placed automatically at the time you set, in your senior's local timezone
  • If the call isn't answered, we'll attempt one retry approximately 15 minutes later. If there's still no answer, the family is notified
  • Your senior can decline any individual call by simply saying so when answering — no call will be forced or repeated against their wishes that day
  • Calls are not recorded as audio. A text transcript is generated for the purpose of creating that day's brief and maintaining conversational memory

5. Accuracy of the brief

The daily brief is generated using AI based on the conversation transcript. While we aim for warmth and accuracy, the brief is a summary, not a verbatim or clinical record. It should not be relied upon as a complete or medically accurate account of your parent's health or wellbeing. If anything in a brief concerns you, we encourage direct follow-up with your parent or their healthcare provider.
